UNIVERSAL HUMAN VALUES

Satya & Prema: The Twin Pillars

In Indian philosophy, Satya (Truth) and Prema (Love) are not abstract concepts, but living dimensions of human consciousness and social harmony.

From the unwavering honesty of Satyakāma Jābāla to the fearless love of Savitri confronting Death, this website presents 7 distinct philosophical stories presented by 7 group members of Group 13.

1. Satyakāma Jābāla

Truthfulness despite uncertainty

When asked about his unknown lineage by Sage Gautama, young Satyakāma spoke the truth without shame or hesitation, proving that moral truthfulness defines true seekerhood.

2. King Harishchandra

Truth despite suffering

Tested by Sage Vishwamitra, King Harishchandra gave up his kingdom, family, and freedom, enduring manual labor at the cremation ground to keep his promise of absolute Truth.

Uncompromising Satya Fortitude Duty

STORY 03 Katha Upanishad

3. Nachiketa & Yama

Courage to seek the Truth

Standing before Death himself, young Nachiketa rejected worldly riches, kingdoms, and sensory pleasures, demanding only the supreme Truth of the Atman (Self).

Viveka Fearlessness Shreyas

STORY 04 Bhāgavata Purāṇa

4. King Rantideva

Selfless Sacrifice & Compassion

After a 48-day fast during a drought, Rantideva gave away his last drop of water to a dying stranger, praying to bear all creatures' suffering instead of seeking personal heaven.

Sarva-bhūta-hita Pure Prema Karuṇā

STORY 05 Mahabharata Tradition

5. Shibi and the Dove

Righteousness & Duty to Protect

When a frightened dove sought shelter, King Shibi offered an equal weight of his own flesh to a hunting hawk to protect the refuge-seeker without causing starvation.

Sharaṇāgati Dharma Sacrifice

STORY 06 Bhāgavata Purāṇa

6. Krishna & Sudama

Pure Friendship (EROS vs PREMA)

King Krishna washed the feet of his impoverished childhood friend Sudama and cherished a handful of simple poha, showing that true love (Prema) transcends wealth and status.

Sakhya-Bhava Prema over Eros Humility

COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS

Philosophical Foundations Matrix

How each of the 7 stories addresses core aspects of Indian Philosophy and Universal Human Values (UHV):

Story / Topic Primary Value Core Challenge Philosophical Resolution
1. Satyakāma Jābālā Truth (Satya) Unknown lineage & social prejudice Honesty defines true character and wisdom
2. King Harishchandra Absolute Truth Severe suffering & loss of kingdom Truthfulness is binding even in extreme adversity
3. Nachiketa & Yama Discernment (Viveka) Material temptations & mortality Choosing Shreyas (Truth) over Preyas (Pleasure)
4. King Rantideva Universal Compassion 48-day starvation during drought Sarva-bhūta-hita: Selfless love for all beings
5. Shibi & the Dove Protection (Sharaṇāgati) Ethical conflict between hawk & dove Sacrificing own flesh to fulfill moral duty
6. Krishna & Sudama Prema over Eros Disparity in social status & wealth Pure spiritual friendship transcends ego & riches
7. Savitri & Satyavan Wisdom & Determination Confronting inevitable death Truthful speech & devotion transform destiny
