STORY 07 • THE MAHABHARATA (VANA PARVA)

SAVITRI & SATYAVAN

A timeless story of love, courage, wisdom and unwavering determination.

Explore the Story ↓

“True love is not merely the bond between two people; it is the courage to stand together against adversity.”

AN ANCIENT TALE

A story beyond love

The story of Savitri and Satyavan appears in the Vana Parva of the Mahabharata. It tells the story of a woman whose wisdom, devotion and determination enabled her to confront Yama, the lord of death.

More than a story of marital devotion, it explores Dharma, Truth, Courage, Love and Human Determination.

THE STORY

From destiny to determination

01

Savitri chooses Satyavan

Savitri, the daughter of King Ashwapati, chooses Satyavan as her husband despite learning that he is destined to die within a year.

02

The day arrives

One year after their marriage, Satyavan goes into the forest with Savitri. There, his predicted death occurs.

03

Savitri follows Yama

Yama appears and takes Satyavan's soul. Savitri follows him, speaking with wisdom, humility and remarkable determination.

04

The power of wisdom

Impressed by Savitri's conduct and wisdom, Yama grants her several boons.

05

The final boon

Savitri asks for the restoration of her family line. Her request creates a situation in which Satyavan's life must also be restored.

06

Satyavan returns

Savitri returns to Satyavan and finds him alive. Her perseverance has overcome what appeared to be an unavoidable fate.

THE JOURNEY

When love walked beyond fear

1

Choice

Savitri chooses Satyavan with full awareness of his destiny.

2

Commitment

She remains beside him despite knowing the danger ahead.

3

Loss

Satyavan dies, fulfilling the prophecy.

4

Confrontation

Savitri follows Yama and engages him through wisdom rather than force.

5

Restoration

Savitri's intelligence and perseverance ultimately lead to Satyavan's return.

THE CONVERSATION

Savitri & Yama

🌸 Savitri

She does not fight Yama physically. Instead, she uses reason, humility, truth and wisdom.

VS

⚖️ Yama

He represents the inevitable law of death and Dharma, testing Savitri's wisdom and resolve.

The lesson: Wisdom and righteousness can be more powerful than physical strength.
