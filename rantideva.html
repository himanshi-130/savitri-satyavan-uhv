STORY 04 • BHĀGAVATA PURĀṆA TRADITION

KING RANTIDEVA

Selfless Sacrifice & Universal Compassion: The king who prayed not for royal status, but to bear the suffering of all living beings.

Explore the Story ↓

“I do not desire liberation, nor supreme powers, nor heavenly realms. My only prayer is to take upon myself the suffering of all beings so that they may live free from distress.”

PURANIC WISDOM

Pinnacle of Selfless Love & Karuṇā

Recorded in the 9th Canto of the Bhāgavata Purāṇa, King Rantideva represents the highest expression of Selfless Love (Prema) and Universal Compassion (Karuṇā) in Indian Philosophy.

Renowned for giving away his wealth to the needy, Rantideva went without food for 48 consecutive days during a severe drought. When food finally arrived, he gave it all away to hungry strangers and animals before touching a morsel himself.

THE NARRATIVE

The Ultimate Prayer of Compassion

01

The Generous King

King Rantideva distributed all his royal wealth, possessions, and food to the poor, living in voluntary poverty alongside his subjects.

02

The 48-Day Fast

A catastrophic drought struck the region. Rantideva and his family fasted for 48 consecutive days, experiencing agonizing hunger and thirst.

03

The Arrival of Food

On the 49th morning, a small portion of milk, ghee, and porridge was brought to him. As he prepared to break his fast, a guest arrived.

04

The Guest & The Outcast

First a hungry guest arrived, then an outcast man, and Rantideva happily gave away portions of food to each without hesitation.

05

The Dogs & The Thirsty Man

Next came a man with hungry dogs, followed by a dying, parched traveller. Rantideva offered his remaining water, uttering his immortal prayer of selfless love.

06

Divine Revelation

The guests revealed themselves as Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva testing his pure heart. They blessed him, and all his subjects became great yogis of universal love.
