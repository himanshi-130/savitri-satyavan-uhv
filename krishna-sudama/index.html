A TIMELESS BOND

When Friendship Transcends Everything

The friendship of Krishna and Sudama is one of the most beautiful examples of unconditional love in Indian tradition. Their relationship reminds us that true friendship is not measured by wealth, position or social status, but by trust, affection and mutual respect.

THEIR JOURNEY

The Story of Krishna & Sudama

01

Students at Sandipani Ashram

Krishna and Sudama studied together at the ashram of Guru Sandipani. Although they came from different backgrounds, they formed a deep and genuine friendship.

02

Life Takes Different Paths

Krishna eventually became the powerful ruler of Dwarka, while Sudama lived a simple and difficult life. Yet, neither wealth nor circumstances changed their bond.

03

The Visit to Dwarka

Encouraged by his wife, Sudama went to meet Krishna. He carried a humble gift of poha as an offering for his childhood friend.

04

A Friend's Embrace

Krishna welcomed Sudama with immense affection. He did not see a poor man before him—he saw his beloved friend. His love remained unchanged.

05

Help Without Humiliation

Sudama never directly asked Krishna for wealth. Krishna understood his friend's circumstances and helped him without making him feel inferior.

INDIAN PHILOSOPHICAL PERSPECTIVE

Love & Truth

प्रेम

PREMA — Love

In Indian philosophical thought, love is more than emotional attachment. It can be understood as a relationship founded upon care, trust, respect and concern for the well-being of another.

"True friendship gives without keeping an account."
सत्य

SATYA — Truth

Truth represents authenticity and living according to what is genuine and right. Krishna and Sudama's friendship does not depend upon appearances or material possessions.

"What is true remains unchanged despite circumstances."
